Kontor is a sovereign intelligence platform for financial-crime work. Your team screens names against sanctions and PEP data on software that runs entirely on your own servers — nothing leaves your environment, and every decision stays with your people.

§ 01 — Purpose

For firms that cannot send client data away.

Smaller regulated institutions carry the same AML obligations as the large banks, without the same resources — and without the option of shipping sensitive data to an outside platform. Kontor runs where your data already lives, and is delivered as four parts on one engine.

FondacoData engine

Resolves people and companies into a single, source-stamped record. Every fact traces to where it came from.

FaktorAI layer

Explains matches and connections in plain language. You bring your own AI model; it stays inside your walls.

KammerInvestigation screens

Where your team reviews, confirms or clears alerts and records the decision — with a four-eyes control and an audit log.

KonvoiSelf-hosted delivery

A sealed package that installs on your own servers, offline if required. Nothing external is needed to keep it running.

§ 03 — Principles
Sovereignty is an architecture, not a clause in a contract.

Sovereign by design

Data and encryption keys stay under European control, on your own servers. It is built that way, not merely promised.

It outlives the founder

Kontor is self-hosted and yours. If the founder disappeared tomorrow, it would keep running on your infrastructure. Source-code escrow is available on request.

The decision stays yours

Kontor is software, not a service. It surfaces and records; your compliance team judges. Statedge never runs screening on a client's behalf.
